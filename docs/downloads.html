Versions

Each release keeps the numbers of the one before. A dataset added later is processed on its own with the same pipeline, reference and settings, so nothing already released is recalculated.

  • v1.1 2026-10-31: Adds two tissue datasets: Moyo et al. 2020 (15 patients, every leiomyoma MED12 mutant) and Leistico et al. 2021 (20 patients, MED12-mutant and other leiomyomas, with a comparison limited to patients without hormone treatment). The v1.0 numbers are unchanged.
  • v1.0 2026-10-30: First release: gene TPM and differential expression for Zuberi et al. 2023 (10 patients) and Buyukcelebi et al. 2023 (MED12-edited cells in 2D and 3D culture).

Columns

  • gene_id, gene_name: Ensembl ID and symbol (GENCODE v44).
  • Counts tables: one column per sample; the read counts DESeq2 was given (adjusted for each sample's transcript lengths, rounded). Use them to rerun or extend the analysis within a dataset.
  • TPM tables: one column per sample, named as in the sample table; values compare genes within a sample and samples within a dataset, not across datasets.
  • baseMean: mean normalized count over all samples in the dataset.
  • log2FC_shrunk: log2 fold change, first group relative to the second, with noisy estimates from low-count genes pulled toward zero. This is the value the site shows. log2FoldChange is the raw estimate, lfcSE its standard error.
  • pvalue, padj: the p-value and the p-value adjusted for the number of genes tested. padj is empty for genes left out of the adjustment because their counts were low.

The Genes page can also download the TPM of the genes it shows, and the Comparisons page its table as filtered. The code that produced everything is on GitHub.
