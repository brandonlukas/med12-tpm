    How to read these plots
    • Each point is one sample. In the tissue panel a gray line joins each patient's myometrium and leiomyoma.
    • The y axis is TPM on a log scale, with the ticks in TPM. It always reaches at least 10 TPM, so a gene that is barely expressed looks that way.
    • Percentile: where the group's mean TPM ranks among all protein-coding genes in that group. The 50th is a typical gene, the 90th is in the top 10%.
    • log2FC and padj are DESeq2 results within each dataset. log2FC is the first group relative to the second; estimates from genes with few counts are pulled toward zero. A change means padj < 0.05 and at least 2-fold (|log2FC| ≥ 1), as on the Comparisons page. Brackets mark changes by padj: * < 0.05, ** < 0.01, *** < 0.001; <2× means padj < 0.05 but less than 2-fold, ns not significant. "Not tested" means too few counts.
    • With several genes, a star above a group marks a change against the reference group (MM or WT); hover it for the exact values. Comparisons that are not against the reference are in the table only.
    • Hovering a row in a table, or one of its entries, lights up the points it refers to.
    • Values are comparable within a dataset, not between datasets.
