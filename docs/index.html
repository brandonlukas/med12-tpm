MED12 RNA-seq Explorer

Overview Genes Comparisons Methods

A gene-level view of three public RNA-seq studies of MED12-mutant uterine fibroids, reprocessed together: tumours with each patient's own myometrium, and myometrial cells engineered to carry a mutation at the same codon of MED12, grown in 2D and as 3D spheroids.

    40 samples · 3 datasets · 9 comparisons · 62,298 GENCODE v44 genes

    Patients

    Ten women; from each, a leiomyoma and the myometrium next to it. Six tumours carry the MED12 G44D mutation, four are MED12 wild-type.

    Zuberi et al., JCI Insight 2023 · GSE204969

    MED12 codon 44

    Mutated in about 70% of uterine fibroids, nearly always in exon 2 and most often at codon 44 (Mäkinen et al., Science 2011). The tumours here carry G44D; the engineered cells carry G44N.

    Engineered cells

    hTERT-immortalized myometrial smooth muscle cells edited by CRISPR: unedited, MED12 knocked out, or carrying the codon-44 mutation (two clones). Grown in 2D, and as spheroids.

    Buyukcelebi et al., Nat Commun 2023 · GSE226014 · GSE226016

    Reading across datasets

    Because every sample went through the same pipeline, values are directly comparable within each dataset, and genes can be compared with each other because the unit is TPM. Expression levels are not comparable between datasets: tissue and cultured cells differ in material, library preparation and read length. To relate the studies, compare what changes (fold changes and significance), not how much is expressed.
