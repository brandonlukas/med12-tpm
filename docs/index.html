MED12 RNA-seq Explorer

Overview Genes Comparisons Methods

A gene-level view of three public RNA-seq studies of MED12-mutant uterine fibroids, reprocessed together: tumours with each patient's own myometrium, and myometrial cells engineered to carry a mutation at the same codon of MED12, grown in 2D and as 3D spheroids.

    40 samples · 3 datasets · 9 comparisons · 62,298 GENCODE v44 genes

    The samples

    Each dot is one RNA-seq sample. Grey is each dataset's reference group; vermilion and pink are the groups that carry a MED12 codon-44 mutation.

    Why these three studies

    About 70% of uterine fibroids (leiomyomas) carry a mutation in MED12, most often at codon 44 (Mäkinen et al., Science 2011). The tissue study shows what MED12-mutant and MED12 wild-type fibroids look like next to the same patient's normal myometrium. The two cell studies ask the question the other way round: put a codon-44 mutation into otherwise identical myometrial cells, or remove MED12 altogether, and see what changes, first in ordinary 2D culture and then in spheroids, a 3D format closer to tissue.

    Side by side, they let you ask whether a gene's behaviour in the tumour is reproduced in the model, and whether that depends on how the cells are grown.

    Reading across datasets

    All 40 samples went through one pipeline (nf-core/rnaseq with Salmon, GENCODE v44), so values are directly comparable within each dataset, and genes can be compared with each other because the unit is TPM. Expression levels are not comparable between datasets: tissue and cultured cells differ in material, library preparation and read length. To relate the studies, compare what changes (fold changes and significance), not how much is expressed.
