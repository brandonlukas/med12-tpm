MED12 RNA-seq Explorer

Overview Genes Comparisons Methods

Gene expression across three public RNA-seq studies of MED12-mutant uterine fibroids: tumours with each patient's own myometrium, and myometrial cells engineered to carry a MED12 codon-44 mutation, grown in 2D and as spheroids.

    Try MED12, HMGA2, PGR, or the Mediator kinase module side by side.

    Why these three studies

    About 70% of uterine fibroids carry a mutation in MED12, most often at codon 44 (Mäkinen et al., Science 2011). The tissue study (Zuberi et al., JCI Insight 2023; GSE204969) shows what those tumours look like, mutant and wild-type, against normal myometrium. The cell study (Buyukcelebi et al., Nat Commun 2023; GSE226014, GSE226016) asks the question the other way round: put a codon-44 mutation (G44N) into myometrial cells, or remove MED12 altogether, and see what changes. Side by side, they show whether a gene's behaviour in the tumour is reproduced in the model, and whether that depends on how the cells are grown.

    All 40 samples went through one pipeline, so values are comparable within each dataset and genes can be compared with each other (the unit is TPM). Between datasets they are not: tissue and cultured cells differ in material and library preparation. Across the studies, compare what changes, not how much is expressed.
