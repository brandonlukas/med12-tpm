    Agreement between two comparisons

    Shrunken log2FC of every gene tested in both, e.g. whether the change in MED12-mutant tumours matches the change in MED12-mutant cells. Uses the significance thresholds above.

    Notes

    • log2FC is the first group relative to the second, shrunken with apeglm: genes with few counts or high variance are pulled toward zero, so the volcano and rankings are led by robust changes. The table also shows the unshrunken estimate. p-values are DESeq2 Wald tests and are the same either way.
    • Rank orders the tested genes by p-value (1 = most significant). Genes removed by the expression filter, or whose padj DESeq2 left empty (independent filtering), are listed without padj and are not plotted.
    • Points at the top edge of the volcano have padj below the smallest value shown (drawn as triangles).
