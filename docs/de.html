    Each gene's fold change in one comparison against its fold change in another, for example whether what changes in MED12-mutant tumors also changes in MED12-mutant cells.

    Notes on the statistics
    • log2FC is the first group relative to the second. Estimates from genes with few counts or noisy data are pulled toward zero, so the volcano and the significance calls are led by robust changes; the CSV also has the raw estimate. The p-values, and so the ranks, do not depend on this.
    • Rank orders the tested genes by p-value, 1 being the most significant. Mean count is the gene's average normalized count over all samples in the dataset.
    • Genes with too few counts to test are not listed; the full tables are in the downloads on the Methods page. A gene with a p-value but no padj was left out of the p-value adjustment because its counts were low.
    • The volcano's y axis is capped so that a few extreme genes do not squash the rest; triangles along the top are genes above the cap. Drag to zoom in; double-click or Reset zoom to come back.
    • In the agreement plot, the rank correlation is over every gene tested in both comparisons, significant or not.
    • The tissue comparisons by genotype use 6 mutant and 4 wild-type patients; the spheroid wild-type group has 2 replicates.
