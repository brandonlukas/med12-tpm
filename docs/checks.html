Methods › Sample checks

Reprocessing the Moyo 2020 and Leistico 2021 tissue samples turned up three people who appear in more than one study, and two Moyo tumors whose reads disagree with the MED12 genotype listed in the paper. Both can be checked from the public reads. Neither changes any result, because each dataset is analyzed on its own.

Patients in more than one study

The 45 tumor–myometrium pairs in the three tissue studies come from 41 people. Inherited variants in the myometrium reads match for five pairs of samples, which make three people:

These five pairs sit 7 to 11 standard deviations above the other pairs between the same two studies; the next highest of all 990 pairs sits at 3. The patient tables agree where they overlap: Zuberi and Moyo use the same patient numbers (1123, 967), and Moyo PT1113 and Leistico pt11 are both G44D, age 51, untreated.

One suspicion did not hold: Leistico pt10 and Zuberi 858 have similar tumor expression, but their variants agree no more than any unrelated pair (0.23).

MED12 genotypes that differ from the paper

Of the 20 tumors with a MED12 codon 44 mutation and enough reads to call, 18 match the published genotype. Two Moyo tumors do not:

TumorMoyo et al. 2020 (Suppl. Table 3)Reads spanning codon 44Other evidence
PT1123G44S10 of 11 read GAT, G44D; none G44SThe same person is G44D in Zuberi 2023 (1123) and Leistico 2021 (pt12)
PT1151G44S5 of 7 read GTT, G44V; none G44SNot in another study

For PT1123, three independent sources agree on G44D. PT1151 rests on the reads alone. Both tumors are MED12 mutant either way, so no grouping or result changes; only the label does. As controls, Leistico's L36R, FH-deficient and HMGA2-high tumors all read wild-type at codon 44.

How they were checked

Same person. For each myometrium sample, 8 million reads were aligned to the 3,000 genes most expressed in myometrium, and inherited variants were called across all 45 samples together. For every pair of samples, the agreement is how often their genotypes match at variant sites both cover well. Myometrium is used because it carries only inherited variants, not the tumor's own mutations.

MED12 genotype. In every tumor's raw reads, the reads spanning MED12 codon 44 (chrX:71,119,403–405, GRCh38) were counted and the three bases read directly: GGT is wild-type, GAT G44D, AGT G44S, GTT G44V, TGT G44C, CGT G44R.

Both checks are scripts in the repository, with their full output: same_patient.py and every pair's agreement; med12_codon44.py and every tumor's codon 44 reads.
